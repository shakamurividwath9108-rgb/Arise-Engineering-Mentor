What the two roadmaps get right—and how ARISE adapts them
IITK Programming Club

Strongest CP resource: a deep Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced topic ladder with explanations, practice problems, books, and videos. Use it as a reference library; do not try to finish its entire advanced syllabus in one sprint.

IITK Students’ Placement Office

Useful practice loop: study one topic, solve level-wise questions, take contests or mock tests, review weak areas, and discuss problems with peers. Its older SDE plan is a guide, so keep links and language choices flexible.

Reddit four-year checklist

Good reminders about academics, projects, contests, internships and communication. It is a community post for CSE/IT and piles on Java, web, DevOps, cloud and system design; comments also warn that following all of it at once is unrealistic. ARISE keeps the breadth but sequences it around your AI & Data Science degree.

A SMART FOUR-YEAR RUNWAY

When to learn what

YEAR 01 · NOW

Programming + math habits

Get strong at C, then Python; build algebra, functions, calculus intuition, probability basics and daily problem-solving.

YEAR 02

Core CS + data foundations

Study DSA, databases, SQL, linear algebra and statistics alongside your college curriculum. Keep notes and solve topic-wise questions.

YEAR 03

DA subjects + past questions

Learn ML and AI after the math; map every official syllabus bullet to a lesson, notes page and practice set.

YEAR 04 · EXAM YEAR

Revision + timed mocks

Use the then-current official syllabus, past papers, full mocks, an error log and spaced revision. Update this runway when your target year is known.
